You can download Hydrogen from
 [http://www.hydrogen-music.org](http://www.hydrogen-music.org).
 On the [Downloads](http://hydrogen-music.org/downloads/)
 page you can find several binaries (installers) for macOS, Windows, and
 Linux.
:::note
From **MacOS Big Sur** onwards you may
 require some extra steps to run Hydrogen on you computer. [This
 page](https://github.com/hydrogen-music/hydrogen/wiki/Running-Hydrogen-on-macOS)
 of the Hydrogen Wiki will guide you through this process step by step.
:::

16.2. MIDI-learnable Widgets
## 16.2. MIDI-learnable Widgets

MIDI Events can be mapped to Actions in the dedicated table at the bottom of the [MIDI tab](ch16.md#chpt.midi.actions) of the Preferences Dialog. For more convenient handling some of these Actions are associated with GUI elements and can directly be mapped to MIDI Events by pressing **Shift** while *left-clicking* the widget.
       

Such elements are:

- 
the [volume faders](ch13s02.md#chpt.mixer.component_strips.fader_and_lcd) in the Instrument Channel Strips

- 
the [FX volume knobs](ch13.md#chpt.mixer.channel_strips.fx_volume) in the Instrument Channel Strips

- 
the [pan rotaries](ch13.md#chpt.mixer.channel_strips.pan) in the Instrument Channel Strips

- 
the [Master Fader Strip](ch13s04.md) in the Mixer

A 'Waiting for MIDI input...' popup informs you that Hydrogen is now waiting for you to press 
       a key or turn/move a controller on your MIDI device.
       

:::note
![[Note]](/img/docs/admonitions/note.svg) Note If the element that does not support MIDI automation, a different popup will inform you.
:::

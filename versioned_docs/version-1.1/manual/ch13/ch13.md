The Mixer window can be opened by pressing <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>M</kbd>, by selecting Mixer in the [View](../ch06/ch06s05.md) option of the Main Menu, or by clicking the Mixer button on the [Main Toolbar](../ch07/ch07s06.md).


The Mixer consists of 3 sections (from left to right): the one containing the [Instrument Channel Strips](#chpt.mixer.channel_strips) and [Component Channel Strips](../ch13/ch13s02.md), 
the [FX Plugin Rack](../ch13/ch13s03.md), and the [Master Fader Strip](../ch13/ch13s04.md). The Hydrogen Mixer works very
much like a hardware mixer does: it lets you set the volume, pan, FX and several 
other things for every instrument as well as the volume of all of these sources mixed together.

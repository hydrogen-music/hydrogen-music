Clicking

![](/img/docs/generated_1.1/btn_jack_transport_off.png)
will enable JACK transport. If disabled, Hydrogen will still use the JACK driver and registered audio ports. But it decouples its own transport state from the one of the JACK server and will not be in sync with other clients anymore.


Using

![](/img/docs/generated_1.1/btn_jack_master_off.png)
Hydrogen can be registered as JACK Timebase Master and will send additional information, like the current speed, to other JACK
clients. Else it will either act as *slave* in the presence of another
Timebase Master, like **Ardour**, or as a stand-alone client in the
absence of a Master.


:::tip
![[Tip]](/img/docs/admonitions/tip.svg) Tip You can check whether Hydrogen is in *slave* mode by hovering over the disabled [Beat Counter](ch07s02.md#sect.main_toolbar.tap_tempo_beat_counter.beat_counter) or [Timeline](ch08s04.md).
:::

:::note
![[Note]](/img/docs/admonitions/note.svg) Note The support of JACK Timebase can disabled in the [Audio tab](ch05s02.md) of the Preferences. This can be useful in the presence of a fault Master broadcasting nuisance information.
:::

The Sample Editor allows you to tweak and manipulate your
samples. It can be accessed by selecting a layer in the [Instrument Editor](../ch11/ch11s02.md) and clicking the
![](/img/docs/generated_en/editLayer_off.png)
button. It will really speed up the creation of a drumkit since
you can do the fine-tuning of the [samples](../go01.md#def.sample) within Hydrogen and avoid a lengthy import-test-tweak workflow using an external application. This mean less switching between Hydrogen and your audio editor
and more time to make music!

:::note
![[Note]](/img/docs/admonitions/note.svg) Note The changes you make to your samples in the Sample Editor are **non-destructive ** and are saved **per song** . So, the original sample will not be changed, and you can reuse the same sample in multiple songs with different Sample Editor settings.
:::

![Figure 10.1. The Soundlibrary](/img/docs/generated_en/SoundLibrary.png)

The Sound Library helps you manage your drumkits,
favourite patterns, and favourite songs. When making new songs or drum
kits, it allows you to reuse and mix instruments and patterns from other kits and songs.


It is located in the Instrument Rack in the lower right corner of Hydrogen, which can be shown or hidden via the [View](../ch06/ch06s05.md) option in the Main Menu or via the [corresponding button](../ch07/ch07s06.md) in the Main Toolbar. The Instrument Rack does either show the [Instrument Editor](../ch11/ch11.md) or the Sound Library depending on what is chosen in the [View](../ch06/ch06s05.md) option in the Main Menu or the corresponding buttons at the top of the Instrument Rack.

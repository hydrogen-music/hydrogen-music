![Figure 13.3. The Component Channel Strip in the Mixer](/img/docs/generated_1.1/MixerComponentStrip.png)

      Right of the [Instrument Channel Strips](ch13.md#chpt.mixer.channel_strips) there additional strips corresponding to the instrument [components](go01.md#def.component).
      

:::note
![[Note]](/img/docs/admonitions/note.svg) Note These channels will not be exposed as JACK output ports when enabling the [Create per-instrument outputs](ch05s02.md#sect.preferences.jack.per_instrument_outs) option in the Preferences.
:::

- 

            ![](/img/docs/generated_en/btn_mute_on.png) : [mutes](go01.md#def.mute) the instrument.
          

- 

            ![](/img/docs/generated_en/btn_solo_on.png) : solos the instrument.
          

- 

            **Fader and LCD**:
              the fader next to the component's name allows you to adjust the volume of all layers (of all instruments) associated with this component. In the background of the fader a volume unit (VU) meter is included representing the instrument's signal level. The LCD displays shows its peak value.
          

:::tip
![[Tip]](/img/docs/admonitions/tip.svg) Tip You can adjust the falloff speed of the peak values in the LCD display via the [Appearance](ch05s05.md#fig.preferences.appearance_tab) tab in the Preferences.
:::

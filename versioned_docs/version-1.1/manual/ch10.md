## Chapter 10. Sound Library (Drumkit/Pattern/Song Manager)

**Table of Contents**
[10.1. Drumkits](ch10.html#chpt.sound_library.drumkits)[10.2. Songs](ch10s02.html)[10.3. Patterns](ch10s03.html)

> ****Figure 10.1. The Soundlibrary****

The Sound Library helps you manage your drumkits,
      favourite patterns, and favourite songs. When making new songs or drum
      kits, it allows you to reuse and mix instruments and patterns from other kits and songs.
      

	    It is located in the Instrument Rack in the lower right corner of Hydrogen, which can be shown or hidden via the [View](ch06s05.html) option in the Main Menu or via the [corresponding button](ch07s06.html) in the Main Toolbar. The Instrument Rack does either show the [Instrument Editor](ch11.html) or the Sound Library depending on what is chosen in the [View](ch06s05.html) option in the Main Menu or the corresponding buttons at the top of the Instrument Rack.
      

## 10.1. Drumkits

The list of drumkits comprises both the kits present at system level and the ones at user level.

          The **System drumkits** are those installed with your package manager or when [compiling](ch02.html) Hydrogen manually. Their location is determined by the compile-time prefix. On
        Unix-like operating systems, this is usually /usr/share/hydrogen/data/drumkits, or
        possibly /usr/local/share/hydrogen/data/drumkits.
        These kits are available to all users on the system, and you usually have no write access to them.

:::tip
![[Tip]](/img/docs/admonitions/tip.svg) Tip You can still modify those kids by saving a tweaked version as a user level kit.
:::

The **User drumkits** are those which you can manage yourself. They
        are stored in the Hydrogen data
        directory (usually $HOME/.hydrogen/data/) within the drumkits
        folder. When you have
        created a new drumkit via [New](ch06s03.html#sect.main_menu.drumkits.new) in the Drumkit option of the Main Menu, you can save it using [Save As](ch06s03.html#sect.main_menu.drumkits.save_as) from the same menu. In addition, you can also [Online Import](ch06s03.html#sect.main_form.online_import_drumkit) a number of drumkits provided by the dev team of Hydrogen.
        

:::warning
![[Warning]](/img/docs/admonitions/warning.svg) Warning If you are using Hydrogen for commercial purposes, (creating songs and selling these online or in any other way) you need to pay special attention to the license type of the drumkit(s) you are using. If the exact license is not available for a drumkit, do **not** assume that it is a [Creative Commons (CC)](https://creativecommons.org/about/cclicenses/) or other open and free license. Even if the kit is CC licensed you should always check with the author before using the kit in your songs. DISCLAIMER : Hydrogen is not responsible for the drumkits that are made available for download by our users, either via the [hydrogen-music.org](hydrogen-music.org) or via any other channel.
:::

To **load a drumkit** via the Sound Library, *right-click* the drumkit and select
       `Load`.  This will replace your current drumkit
       with the one that you selected.  To **load a single instrument** from that
       kit, *left-click* the ▶ to the left of the
       drumkit's name to show all the instruments.  With your left mouse
       button, *click* and *drag* the instrument into your current kit.  The
       instrument will be added to the drumkit that you currently have
       loaded.

7.3. BPM Control and Metronome
## 7.3. BPM Control and Metronome
![|Part of the Main Toolbar responsible for the tempo settings. To the left there are on top two buttons for decreasing and increasing the tempo and below one to activate the metronome. To the right and covering most of the area is a LCD showing the current BPM. At the far right there is a tall button activating the recalculation of samples processed by Rubber Band at tempo changes.](/img/docs/generated_en/background_BPM.png)
Set the speed of the song in the range from 10 to 400 bpm (beats per minute).
        

:::tip
![[Tip]](/img/docs/admonitions/tip.svg) Tip You can use the *mouse wheel* to decrease and increase the value of this widget.
:::

In addition you can use
          ![](/img/docs/generated_1.1/btn_metronome_off.png)
          to toggle the metronome.

:::tip
![[Tip]](/img/docs/admonitions/tip.svg) Tip The volume of the metronome can be adjusted in the [Audio tab](ch05s02.md) of the Preferences.
:::

	      If either Rubber Band support is compiled into your current version of Hydrogen or correctly configured in the [General tab](ch05.md#chpt.preferences.general_tab) of the Preferences, you will also find the
          ![](/img/docs/generated_1.1/rubber_off.png)
          button next to the BPM widget. Activating it will enable recalculation of all sample pitches using Rubber Band whenever the tempo of the song will be changed (see [Section 12.2](ch12s02.md) for details.
